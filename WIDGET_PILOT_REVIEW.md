## The three

**1. Waves (Science AQA, Physics Paper 2 L6)** — sits after the wave
equation, before refraction. Wave speed is FIXED at 6 m/s (a property of
the medium), so dragging frequency visibly shortens the wavelength while
v = f×λ stays balanced — the point students most often miss. Amplitude is
separate and changes energy, not speed. A toggle switches to a
longitudinal view with compressions and rarefactions.

**2. Storm hydrograph (Geography AQA, Paper 1 L19)** — sits after the
"flashy hydrograph" key fact, before the factors sections it lets you
test. Same storm every time; three sliders (relief, geology, land use)
change the basin. Lag time and peak discharge update live, the limbs are
labelled, and the caption names the outcome in the lesson's own words
("flashy hydrograph… high flood risk").

**3. Sorting algorithms (Computer Science OCR, Computational Thinking L4)**
— sits after the three algorithms are taught, before "Comparing the
algorithms", so students can race them. Step through every comparison and
swap, or press Play. Insertion sort shows the lifted value held above a
dashed gap, exactly as exam diagrams draw it. Comparison and move counters
let students see *why* merge sort wins.

## How they're built (the important bit)

Hand-built components, injected by URL from a map in
`js/lesson-widgets.js` — **not** generated per lesson. This is the
annotated-music-player model: build once, verify once, reuse with data.
The alternative (a model writing a fresh interactive per lesson) is the
Gemini-diagram trap: unverifiable at scale, and a wrong *interactive*
teaches wrong physics rather than merely looking wrong.

Supabase content is untouched, so nothing to un-pick if you say no.

## What I verified

- **Physics numerically**: v = f×λ holds at exactly 6 m/s across the
  frequency range, and T×f = 1. Not eyeballed — measured.
- **Sorting against the real algorithms**: the animation replays a script
  generated by *running* the algorithm, so it cannot drift. All three end
  sorted, and no value is ever lost or duplicated at any intermediate step.
- **Two bugs the audit caught and I fixed**: insertion sort under-reported
  comparisons (3 recorded vs 15 actually performed — bad, since OCR
  students compare algorithms by that number), and it showed a duplicated
  bar where the exam diagram shows a gap.
- **Placement** in all three lessons, **no horizontal overflow at 360px**,
  controls stack cleanly on a phone.
- Canvas-only state is mirrored to a screen-reader live region.
  `prefers-reduced-motion` starts the wave paused.
