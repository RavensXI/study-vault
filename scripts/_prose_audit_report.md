### Cross-Board Trends

Several error types recur systematically across multiple texts and boards:

**1. Fabricated / unverified quotations in quotation marks** — The most widespread error type across the corpus. Affects: Hobson's Choice (13 fabricated quotes including all three 'key quotations' in L5), Blood Brothers ('That Guy' as soliloquy), Journey's End ('It's the only way to forget', 'the one man I could talk to'), An Inspector Calls ('these girls aren't cheap labour — they're people' misattributed to Inspector), Much Ado (condemnified, 'Thou and I are too wise' misattributed to Benedick). Priority fix: any quote in a Key Fact box or flashcard that cannot be found in a standard edition must be verified or removed.

**2. Fabricated / hallucinated plot points** — Leave Taking (abandoned child in Jamaica — does not exist in the play), Boys Don't Cry (Josh presented as supportive boyfriend/symbol of acceptance when he is one of the attackers), Pigeon English (Dell Farm Crew cited as killers when Jordan is named by all sources).

**3. Wrong text being taught** — The Curious Incident: all 8 Eduqas lessons teach Haddon's novel but Eduqas exams the Stephens 2012 play. Refugee Boy: character identities swapped (Ruth = foster sister not caseworker, Mariam = caseworker not mother).

**4. Character identity errors** — My Name Is Leon (Maureen described as Jamaican-British; she is white), Leave Taking (Del/Viv ages inverted, pregnancy wrongly attributed to Viv), Refugee Boy (Ruth and Mariam roles swapped), Coram Boy (Meshak's sacrifice inverted with Thomas Ledbury's).

**5. Cross-text quote contamination** — Jane Eyre Eduqas L8 quotes 'I was in the middle before I knew that I had begun' as a Rochester quote; it is Darcy's line from Pride and Prejudice Ch.60. Animal Farm L8 (AQA) states Minimus's poem replaces the Seven Commandments when it is placed at the opposite end of the barn.

**6. Chapter/scene misattribution in structured texts** — Oranges Are Not the Only Fruit (Melanie relationship assigned to Leviticus not Numbers/Joshua), Sign of Four (chapter numbers shifted one too early from Ch.5 onward), History Boys (Drummer Hodge scene assigned to Oxford interview not Act 1 classroom).
