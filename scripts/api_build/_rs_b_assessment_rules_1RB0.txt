ASSESSMENT RULES — Pearson Edexcel Level 1/Level 2 GCSE (9-1) in Religious Studies B
(1RB0), "Beliefs in Action". Specification Issue 2, January 2024. Every statement
below is quoted or directly summarised from that specification. It is the ONLY
authority on this qualification's exam structure. Anything about the exam that is
not stated here is not established: treat it as a fabrication, however plausible.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. QUALIFICATION SHAPE (verbatim, "Content and assessment overview")
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"The Pearson Edexcel Level 1/Level 2 GCSE (9-1) in Religious Studies B consists of
three Areas of Study from which students study two, which are then assessed through
two externally set examination papers."

"Schools must select a different religion for their two Areas of Study. Schools who
select Catholic Christianity for an Area of Study are not permitted to select
Christianity for their second Area of Study and vice versa."

"Students must complete all assessment in May/June in any single year."

There is NO non-exam assessment, NO coursework and NO controlled assessment in this
qualification. Any claim that students complete a portfolio, a project, fieldwork or
any internally assessed task is FALSE.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
2. THE THREE PAPERS (verbatim)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Paper 1: Area of Study 1 - Religion and Ethics (Paper code 1RB0/1A-1G)
  Written examination: 1 hour and 45 minutes
  50% of the qualification
  102 marks
  One religion must be studied from a choice of seven: 1A Catholic Christianity,
  1B Christianity, 1C Islam, 1D Buddhism, 1E Hinduism, 1F Judaism, 1G Sikhism.
  Students must study all four content sections for the chosen religion:
    - Beliefs
    - Marriage and the Family
    - Living the Religious Life
    - Matters of Life and Death

Paper 2: Area of Study 2 - Religion, Peace and Conflict (Paper code 1RB0/2A-2G)
  Written examination: 1 hour and 45 minutes
  50% of the qualification
  102 marks
  One religion must be studied from a choice of seven: 2A Catholic Christianity,
  2B Christianity, 2C Islam, 2D Buddhism, 2E Hinduism, 2F Judaism, 2G Sikhism.
  Students must study all four content sections for the chosen religion:
    - Beliefs
    - Crime and Punishment
    - Living the Religious Life
    - Peace and Conflict

Paper 3: Area of Study 3 - Religion, Philosophy and Social Justice (Paper code
1RB0/3A-3G)
  Written examination: 1 hour and 45 minutes
  50% of the qualification
  102 marks
  One religion must be studied from a choice of seven: 3A Catholic Christianity,
  3B Christianity, 3C Islam, 3D Buddhism, 3E Hinduism, 3F Judaism, 3G Sikhism.
  Students must study all four content sections for the chosen religion:
    - Beliefs
    - Philosophy of Religion
    - Living the Religious Life
    - Equality

Students sit TWO of these three papers. In practice a centre enters Paper 1 plus
either Paper 2 or Paper 3, on a different religion from Paper 1.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
3. ASSESSMENT OVERVIEW FOR EVERY PAPER (verbatim, identical on all three)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"Students must answer all questions for the chosen religion."
"The assessment consists of four questions. Each question is numbered (a) to (d)."
"The paper includes short open, open response and extended writing questions."
"5% of the marks will be for spelling, punctuation and grammar and use of specialist
terminology (SPaG)."

Also stated per paper:
"Students must answer all sections and all questions on the chosen religion. The
religion must be different for each area of study."
"The paper will assess spelling, punctuation and grammar (SPaG) and use of specialist
terminology and these will contribute a minimum of 5% of marks towards the overall
weighting for this paper."

There is one question per content section, so the four questions on a paper follow
the paper's four content sections in order.

THE SPECIFICATION DOES NOT STATE how the SPaG marks are distributed between
questions, nor how long a student should spend on each part, nor how many marks each
lettered part carries as a table. A lesson must NOT state a SPaG distribution, a
per-part timing, or a per-question mark total that is not derivable from section 4
below. It must not claim a number of lines, a word count, or a number of paragraphs
that an answer must reach.

4. COMMAND WORDS AND THEIR MARKS (verbatim, Appendix 3)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
"The following command words will be used in the assessment of students across all
papers."

  State     - "Provide knowledge of religion and belief by recalling factual
              information."                              3 marks    AO1
  Outline   - "Provide knowledge of religion and belief by recalling factual
              information."                              3 marks    AO1
  Describe  - "Provide an understanding of religion and belief and contrast with
              that of another."                          4 marks    AO1
  Explain   - "There are two ways this command word will be used:
              1. (4 marks) Provide understanding of an aspect of religion and belief
                 and developing this beyond a habitual response.
              2. (5 marks) Provide understanding of an aspect of religion and belief
                 and developing this beyond a habitual response. In addition,
                 students will be required to reference one source of wisdom or
                 authority in support of their explanation."
                                                         4 or 5 marks   AO1
  Evaluate  - "Analyse the statement provided, considering different points of view
              and creating logical chains of reasoning that are underpinned by
              understanding of religion and belief. Reasoned judgements should be
              made, supported by the appraisal of arguments and leading to a
              justified conclusion."                     12 marks   AO2

"All GCSEs must provide opportunities for students to write responses which are of
sufficient length to allow students to demonstrate the ability to construct and
develop a sustained line of reasoning which is coherent, relevant, substantiated and
logically structured. In this GCSE, these abilities are rewarded in the mark schemes
of questions that are assessed using the command word 'Evaluate'. Additional
instructions are provided to students for all 'Evaluate' questions."

REGISTERED QUESTION TARIFFS FOR THIS BUILD (each one is taken straight from the
table above and is therefore CORRECT — do not flag any of them):
  "3 marks - Outline Three"             (Outline, 3 marks, AO1)
  "4 marks - Describe Two"              (Describe, 4 marks, AO1)
  "4 marks - Explain Two"               (Explain, 4 marks, AO1)
  "5 marks - Explain Two with Sources"  (Explain, 5 marks, AO1, must reference one
                                         source of wisdom and authority)
  "12 marks - Discuss a Statement"      (Evaluate, 12 marks, AO2)

A lesson may say that a paper's questions run from a short recall part through to a
12-mark evaluation, and that one part of each question requires a source of wisdom
and authority. It may NOT put a letter against a tariff ("part (b) is worth 4
marks") because the specification does not publish that mapping.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
5. ASSESSMENT OBJECTIVES (verbatim)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
AO1: "Demonstrate knowledge and understanding of religion and belief, including:
      beliefs, practices and sources of authority; influence on individuals,
      communities and societies; similarities and differences within and/or between
      religions and beliefs."                                    50% of the GCSE
AO2: "Analyse and evaluate aspects of religion and belief, including their
      significance and influence."                               50% of the GCSE

Breakdown of assessment objectives: each of Paper 1, Paper 2 and Paper 3 is 25% AO1
and 25% AO2 of the whole GCSE, i.e. each paper splits its own marks 50/50 between
AO1 and AO2. Total for the GCSE: 50% AO1, 50% AO2.

Synoptic assessment: "Synopticity will be assessed where students are required to
bring together their knowledge and understanding of religion from across the course
of study."

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
6. WHAT IS IN SCOPE FOR THIS BUILD
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This build covers, and only covers:
  Area of Study 1 (Paper 1) - Christianity          -> unit area1-christianity
  Area of Study 1 (Paper 1) - Catholic Christianity -> unit area1-catholic-christianity
  Area of Study 2 (Paper 2) - Islam                 -> unit area2-islam
  Area of Study 2 (Paper 2) - Judaism               -> unit area2-judaism

A lesson that teaches Area of Study 3 content (Philosophy of Religion, Equality) as
though it were on the student's paper is out of scope: flag HIGH. A lesson that
teaches a section from the wrong Area of Study for its religion (for example
"Marriage and the Family" inside an Area of Study 2 unit, or "Crime and Punishment"
inside an Area of Study 1 unit) is out of scope: flag HIGH.

The numbered content points quoted in each lesson's section markers are the scope of
that lesson. Content well outside them, taught as assessed material, is a finding.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------
7. LANGUAGE RULES (site policy, not the specification)
------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The exam board is never named in student-facing text: lessons say "the exam board",
"your exam", "this paper". "Pearson", "Edexcel", "AQA", "OCR", "Eduqas" and "WJEC"
must not appear. Paper codes (1RB0, 1RB0/1B), section numbers as spec references
("2.4"), and the board's own level descriptors must not appear either. Mark bands
are the site's own ladder: Mastering / Secure / Developing / Emerging.
Flag any breach as MEDIUM on the field where it appears.
