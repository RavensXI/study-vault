## Findings

### MEDIUM — media-industries L05: PEGI enforceable ratings example

**Lesson:** Regulation, Digital Disruption and the BBFC, OFCOM, IPSO, ASA and PEGI  
**Lesson ID:** `d0e06c8c-50e7-4999-b481-1dfce57e126c`

**Claim in content:**
> "PEGI — Pan European Game Information — provides age rating labels for video games: 3, 7, 12, 16 and 18. In the UK, PEGI ratings became legally enforceable in 2012, meaning that selling an 18-rated game to a person under 18 is a criminal offence."

**Issue:** The example singles out only the PEGI 18 rating, implying it is the (or the only) legally enforceable one. In fact, the Video Recordings (Labelling) Regulations 2012 made PEGI **12, 16, and 18** ratings all legally enforceable. PEGI 3 and 7 remain advisory. A student who only reads this sentence could incorrectly state in an exam that "the PEGI 18 rating is legally enforceable" and miss that 12 and 16 are also covered by law.

**Correction:**
> "In the UK, PEGI 12, 16 and 18 ratings became legally enforceable in 2012 under the Video Recordings (Labelling) Regulations 2012, meaning that selling a 12-, 16- or 18-rated game to a person below those ages is a criminal offence. PEGI 3 and 7 remain advisory."

**Source:** https://www.gov.uk/government/news/new-rules-to-better-protect-children-from-inappropriate-video-game-content

### LOW — media-representations L04: Marie Ruge spelling

**Lesson:** Viewpoints, Self-Representation and the Social Significance of Representation  
**Lesson ID:** `a48da187-9052-43d9-860c-4ebffafee4e0`

**Claim in content:**
> "Scholars Johan Galtung and Marie Ruge identified a set of factors — news values — that make events more likely to be selected for news coverage"

**Issue:** The researcher's first name is "Mari" (short for Mari Holmboe Ruge), not "Marie". The original 1965 paper is "The Structure of Foreign News" by Johan Galtung and Mari Holmboe Ruge, published in the Journal of Peace Research.

**Correction:** Change "Marie Ruge" to "Mari Ruge" (or "Mari Holmboe Ruge").

**Source:** https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/002234336500200104
