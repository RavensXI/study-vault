### L11 — Business Ownership and Sources of Capital
**LOW x2** (both verified accurate):

1. "A private limited company (Ltd) requires formal registration with Companies House, and annual accounts are filed publicly."  
   Verified: Gov.uk confirms both requirements. Private limited companies must register with Companies House; filed accounts are publicly accessible records. No error.

2. "Sole trader: There is no formal registration process beyond informing tax authorities."  
   Technically, HMRC Self Assessment registration is legally required for sole traders earning over £1,000. However, the lesson explicitly includes "informing tax authorities" as the required step — it is not claiming no registration is needed. The contrast being drawn is accurate: sole traders do NOT register with Companies House (unlike Ltd companies). This is the standard R067 specification distinction between the two structures. No mark-affecting error.
