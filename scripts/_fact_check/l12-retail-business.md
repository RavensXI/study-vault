## HIGH — Lesson 2: Franchise grouped with Ltd as providing limited liability

**Key Fact box**: "Limited liability (Ltd, franchise) means the owner only risks the money invested in the business."

Franchise is a business *arrangement*, not a legal structure conferring limited liability. A franchisee's liability depends on the legal form they operate under — if they are a sole trader, they have **unlimited liability**; if they trade through an Ltd, they have limited liability. Grouping franchise with Ltd as automatically limited-liability is wrong and would cause students to mis-answer ownership/liability exam questions.

Additionally, the WJEC L1/2 Retail Business spec (1.1.2) does **not** list franchise as an ownership type. The spec covers: sole trader, partnership, private/public limited company, co-operative, not-for-profit and charity. Any franchise content taught as an ownership form goes beyond spec scope for this qualification.

**Fix**: Remove "franchise" from the limited liability grouping. If franchise is retained as supplementary content, label it clearly as extension material and note that liability depends on the franchisee's chosen legal structure.

## HIGH — Lesson 7: STELE acronym adds "Legal" as an external factor not in the spec

**Key Fact box**: "Remember all five external factor categories with the acronym STELE: Social, Technological, Economic, Legal (covered in class), Environmental and Ethical."

The WJEC spec (1.2.3) specifies exactly five external factors: **Economic, Technological, Social, Ethical, Environmental**. Legal is not among them. STELE has six letters (not five, despite the claim), and teaching "Legal" as a sixth category would lead students to write about factors the examiner does not expect and to mis-count the categories.

**Fix**: Remove Legal. Use a corrected five-factor acronym (e.g. ESTEE or STEEE). Any legal-factor content should be moved to a clearly labelled extension note.

## HIGH — Lesson 7: Black Friday described as "the last Friday in November"

Black Friday falls on the **fourth Friday of November** (the day after US Thanksgiving, fixed to the fourth Thursday of November). In years when November has five Fridays, the fourth and last Fridays differ. Stating "last Friday" is factually incorrect in those years.

**Fix**: "Black Friday falls on the fourth Friday in November (the day after US Thanksgiving)."

## MEDIUM — Lesson 2: Small business size band missing lower bound in body text

Body text says "small businesses (up to 49 employees)" — this omits the lower bound, implying micro-sized businesses (0–9) are also "small." The Key Fact in the same lesson correctly states 10–49.

**Fix**: Change prose to "small businesses (10–49 employees)."

## MEDIUM — Lesson 5: Competitive methods list does not match spec terminology

The lesson lists seven methods (price, product quality and range, customer service, convenience, location, loyalty schemes, branding). The WJEC spec (1.2.1) lists: cheaper prices, quality products, wider range, customer service, advertising, promotional activities, design of store, use of multi-channels, staff training. Several spec-mandated terms (advertising, promotional activities, design of store, multi-channels, staff training) are absent; several non-spec terms (convenience, location, loyalty schemes, branding) have been substituted. Students using only the lesson list may miss spec-expected answers on knowledge-recall questions.

**Fix**: Align method labels with spec wording. Loyalty schemes, branding and location can appear as examples within the spec categories rather than as standalone items.
