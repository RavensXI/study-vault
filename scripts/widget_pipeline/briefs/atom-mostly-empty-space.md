# Widget brief: The atom is mostly empty space

Cluster id: `atom-mostly-empty-space`  |  serves 5 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

An atom is not a solid, densely packed ball, it consists of a tiny dense nucleus surrounded by mostly empty space occupied by electrons.

> The Structure of the Atom An atom is the smallest part of an element that can exist. Atoms are extraordinarily small &mdash; a typical atomic radius is about \(1 \times 10^{-10}\) m. Each atom has a tiny, dense nucleus at its centre. The nucleus contains two types of sub-atomic particle: protons (positive charge, relative mass 1) and neutrons (no charge, relative mass 1). Electrons occupy energy levels around the nucleus and have a negative charge. Their relative mass is so small &mdash; roughly one part in 1836 that of a proton &mdash; that it is treated as negligible. Key Fact Relative charges and approximate relative masses: Proton &mdash; charge +1, mass 1. Neutron &mdash; charge 0, mass 1. Electron &mdash; charge &minus;1, mass negligible.
