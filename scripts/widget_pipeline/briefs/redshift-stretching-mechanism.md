# Widget brief: Redshift is not light being physically stretched by a force

Cluster id: `redshift-stretching-mechanism`  |  serves 2 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Redshift arises because the wavelength of light is lengthened as the source recedes or as space expands, not because the light itself is being pulled or stretched like a rubber band by the galaxy.

> The Expanding Universe One of the most profound discoveries in science is that the universe is expanding. In the 1920s, astronomers began measuring the spectra of distant galaxies and noticed something remarkable: the absorption lines in almost every galaxy&rsquo;s spectrum were shifted to longer, redder wavelengths. This redshift indicates that galaxies are moving away from us. The more distant a galaxy, the greater its redshift &mdash; and therefore the faster it is receding. This is not galaxies moving through space; it is space itself that is expanding, carrying galaxies apart like raisins in a rising dough.
