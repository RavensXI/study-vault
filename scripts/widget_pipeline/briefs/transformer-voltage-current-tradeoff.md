# Widget brief: Stepping up voltage in a transformer requires current to drop, conserving power

Cluster id: `transformer-voltage-current-tradeoff`  |  serves 6 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

A transformer that steps up voltage causes current to decrease proportionally, so power (and total energy) is conserved, not increased.

> How Transformers Work A transformer changes the voltage of an alternating current (AC) supply. It has two coils of wire &mdash; the primary coil and the secondary coil &mdash; wound around a shared soft iron core. An alternating current in the primary coil creates a continuously changing magnetic field in the iron core. This changing field induces an alternating voltage in the secondary coil by electromagnetic induction. Transformers only work with AC because a steady (direct) current produces a constant magnetic field that cannot induce a voltage in the secondary coil.
