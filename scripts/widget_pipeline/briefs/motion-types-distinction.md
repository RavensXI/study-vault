# Widget brief: Distinguishing types of motion

Cluster id: `motion-types-distinction`  |  serves 3 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

The four motion types (rotary, linear, reciprocating, oscillating) must be distinguished by how they actually move over time, not just by static description, especially reciprocating vs oscillating.

> What Is Subtractive Manufacturing? Subtractive manufacturing starts with a block, sheet or bar of material and removes unwanted sections until the finished part remains. Every process in this lesson &mdash; sawing, shearing, laser cutting, turning, milling and drilling &mdash; is subtractive . The removed material becomes waste (swarf, chips or offcuts), so material selection and planning the cutting sequence carefully both reduce cost and waste.
