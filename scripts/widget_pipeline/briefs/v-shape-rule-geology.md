# Widget brief: The V-shape rule reflects the 3D geometry of dipping strata

Cluster id: `v-shape-rule-geology`  |  serves 1 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

A downstream-dipping stratum produces an upstream-pointing V on a map because of the 3D geometry of a tilted bed intersecting a valley — the rule is a geometric consequence, not arbitrary.

The Simplified Geological Map Your written examination (the practical-skills component) is entirely based on a simplified geological map . Unlike the full 1:50,000 BGS maps used in professional work, the exam version uses a small number of named rock units, shows faults and fold axes clearly, and is accompanied by a cross-section framework and a stratigraphic column. The skills you need are: reading the map to identify rock types at any point, inferring structure from the map pattern, drawing or completing a cross-section, and constructing a sequence of geological events from the evidence shown.
