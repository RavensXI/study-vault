# Widget brief: Energy is transferred and dispersed, not destroyed, when 'wasted'

Cluster id: `conservation-of-energy-dispersal`  |  serves 8 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Wasted thermal energy is transferred to the surroundings and becomes increasingly spread out (dispersed), but it still exists — it is not destroyed or lost from the universe.

> Energy Stores Energy cannot be created or destroyed — it can only be transferred between different energy stores . You need to know eight energy stores. Key Fact The eight energy stores are: kinetic, thermal, chemical, elastic potential, gravitational potential, electrostatic, magnetic, and nuclear. Kinetic energy depends on the mass and speed of an object. The equation is: $$E_k = \frac{1}{2} m v^2$$ Where \(E_k\) is kinetic energy in joules (J), \(m\) is mass in kilograms (kg), and \(v\) is speed in metres per second (m/s). Gravitational potential energy depends on the mass, height, and gravitational field strength: $$E_p = m g h$$ Where \(E_p\) is gravitational potential energy in joules, \(m\) is mass in kg, \(g\) is gravitational field strength (10 N/kg on Earth), and \(h\) is height in metres. > Levels of Organisation in an Ecosystem An ecosystem is a community of living organisms interacting with each other and their physical environment (B4.1d). Ecosystems range in scale from a small pond to an entire ocean, but all share the same levels of organisation. Individual organisms of the same species living in the same area form a population . Different populations living together form a community . A community interacting with the abiotic (non-living) environment makes up the ecosystem. Within communities, organisms depend on each other — this is interdependence (B4.1f). Key relationships include predation (one organism hunts and eats another), mutualism (both organisms benefit, such as bees pollinating flowers while collecting nectar) and parasitism (one organism benefits while the other is harmed, such as fleas on a dog). > Conservation of Energy The conservation of energy is one of the most important ideas in physics. It states that energy cannot be created or destroyed — only transferred from one store to another. The total energy in a closed system always stays the same. Key Fact Energy cannot be created or destroyed. In a closed system, the total energy before a change is always equal to the total energy after the change.
