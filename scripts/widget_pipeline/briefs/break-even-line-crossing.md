# Widget brief: Break-even depends on which line crosses which and its slope

Cluster id: `break-even-line-crossing`  |  serves 2 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

The break-even point and subsequent profit/loss are determined by the relative positions and slopes of the cost and revenue lines, not simply the point where losses stop.

Understanding break-even is essential for start-up planning, pricing decisions and securing finance. Break-even analysis uses three inputs: the selling price per unit , the variable cost per unit , and the total fixed costs .
