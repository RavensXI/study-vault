## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Reinforced concrete works because steel bars are positioned within the concrete to resist tension, not simply bonded to its surface.

> What Is the Built Environment Life Cycle? Every building, road or piece of infrastructure passes through a sequence of stages from the moment its raw materials are taken from the earth to the point when it is eventually pulled down and its materials are disposed of, reused or recycled. This full sequence is known as the built environment life cycle . Understanding this life cycle helps construction professionals make better decisions about materials, design and maintenance — and is increasingly important as the industry seeks to reduce waste and environmental impact. Stage 1 — Raw Material Extraction Before any construction material can be made, its raw materials must be extracted from the natural environment.
