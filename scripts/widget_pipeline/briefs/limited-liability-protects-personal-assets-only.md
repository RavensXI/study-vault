# Widget brief: Limited liability protects personal assets, not the business from failing

Cluster id: `limited-liability-protects-personal-assets-only`  |  serves 2 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Limited liability means an owner's personal assets are protected if the business fails, but it does not stop the business itself going bust or guarantee no loss

> Why Does Business Structure Matter? The legal structure of a business is not just a paperwork choice &mdash; it determines who is responsible for debts, who makes decisions, and how profits are shared. A sole trader who cannot pay her suppliers risks losing her car, her home and her savings. A shareholder in a private limited company who faces the same crisis loses only the money she invested. That difference is limited liability &mdash; one of the most important concepts in GCSE Business. Key Fact Unlimited liability applies to sole traders and partnerships. This means there is no legal separation between the owner and the business &mdash; personal possessions such as a house or car can be seized by creditors if the business cannot pay its debts.
