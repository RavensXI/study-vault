# Widget brief: Devolution is not the same as independence

Cluster id: `devolution-vs-independence`  |  serves 1 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Devolved nations have specific powers granted by and still ultimately subject to the central government, unlike fully independent countries which have complete sovereign control over their own affairs.

> Why Government Operates at Different Levels The UK is governed at several levels: the UK-wide Westminster Parliament, devolved administrations in Scotland, Wales and Northern Ireland, and elected local councils across England and the other nations. Each level has different responsibilities, different budgets and different electoral systems. This multi-layered structure means that decisions about a school in Cardiff are made differently from decisions about a hospital in Glasgow or a road in Somerset. Understanding which level of government does what is a key citizenship skill. Local Government Local government is delivered by elected councils .
