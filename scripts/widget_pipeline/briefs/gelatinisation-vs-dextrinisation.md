# Widget brief: Gelatinisation vs dextrinisation are different processes

Cluster id: `gelatinisation-vs-dextrinisation`  |  serves 3 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Gelatinisation needs water and thickens, while dextrinisation is a dry heat browning reaction of starch — they are not the same process.
