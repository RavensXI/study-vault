# Widget brief: Colour in film carries narrative meaning

Cluster id: `film-colour-as-narrative-device`  |  serves 1 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Colour choices in cinematography are a deliberate narrative/thematic tool, not incidental decoration.

Deakins, one of the most decorated cinematographers working in English-language cinema, received an Academy Award nomination for Best Cinematography for Skyfall , the first modern Bond entry to achieve this. The score was composed by Thomas Newman; Adele co-wrote and performed the title song, which won the Academy Award for Best Original Song. The film was made for approximately $200 million and grossed over $1.1 billion worldwide, making it the highest-grossing Bond film at the time of its release.
