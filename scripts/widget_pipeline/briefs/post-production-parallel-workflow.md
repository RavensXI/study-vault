# Widget brief: Post-production is parallel, not just editing

Cluster id: `post-production-parallel-workflow`  |  serves 1 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Post-production involves multiple specialists working simultaneously on different tasks, not a single linear editing stage.

> Who Makes Media? Creating a media product &mdash; whether a film, a website or a video game &mdash; is rarely the work of one person. It requires a team of specialists, each responsible for a different aspect of the project. Understanding the key job roles in media production, and the order in which their work happens, is essential both for the exam and for any practical media work you carry out. Creative and Management Roles At the top of a production, the producer manages the budget, schedule and overall delivery of a project. They are responsible for making sure the product is finished on time, within budget and to the client's specification. The director makes the creative decisions: visual style, tone, performances and storytelling choices in film and TV productions.
