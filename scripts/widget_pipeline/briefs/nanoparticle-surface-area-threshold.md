# Widget brief: Nanoparticle properties change at a surface-area threshold

Cluster id: `nanoparticle-surface-area-threshold`  |  serves 4 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Nanoparticle properties don't scale down predictably with size—there's a threshold where surface-area-to-volume ratio becomes large enough to dominate behaviour

To put this in context, a single nanometre is one-billionth of a metre &mdash; a human hair is roughly 80,000 nm wide. Nanoparticles sit between individual atoms (which are typically 0.1&ndash;0.5 nm across) and bulk materials that we handle at everyday scales. When you divide a lump of material into many tiny nanoparticles, the total surface area increases enormously while the total mass stays the same.
