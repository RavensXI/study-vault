# Widget brief: Fractional distillation by boiling point

Cluster id: `fractional-distillation-boiling-point`  |  serves 4 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Fractional distillation separates hydrocarbons by differences in boiling point (vapour condensing at different heights/temperatures), not by physical sieving of molecule sizes.

Crude oil is separated into useful fractions by fractional distillation . The oil is heated so that most of it vaporises. The vapours rise up a tall fractionating column that is hot at the bottom and cool at the top. Different fractions condense at different heights depending on the boiling points of their molecules. Shorter-chain molecules have weaker forces between them and lower boiling points, so they condense near the top; longer-chain molecules with stronger forces between them condense near the bottom.
