# Widget brief: Plot is not the same as story

Cluster id: `plot-vs-story`  |  serves 1 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Story is the full chronological sequence of events in the fictional world; plot is the order and selection of events actually shown to the audience, which can differ from story order.

> Plot and Story Are Not the Same Thing When people say &ldquo;what&rsquo;s the film about?&rdquo; they are really asking two separate questions at once. The first is about the plot &mdash; the order in which the film chooses to show us events. The second is about the story &mdash; the full, chronological sequence of everything that happens in the film&rsquo;s world, including events we never see. Russian literary theorists called these two concepts syuzhet (plot &mdash; the arrangement on screen) and fabula (story &mdash; the underlying chronology). Understanding the gap between them is essential for film analysis, because a director&rsquo;s choice about what to show, when to show it, and what to withhold is a primary tool for creating meaning and shaping spectator response.
