# Widget brief: Vertical vs horizontal integration direction

Cluster id: `vertical-vs-horizontal-integration`  |  serves 1 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Vertical integration moves along the supply chain (different stage), while horizontal integration combines businesses at the same stage — they are not the same thing.

> What Is a Media Conglomerate? When you watch a blockbuster film, stream a television series and read a news website owned by the same parent company, you are experiencing the reach of a conglomerate . In the media industry, a small number of very large organisations control a huge range of products across television, film, music, publishing, gaming, streaming and beyond. Understanding how they grow &mdash; and what that means for the content we consume &mdash; is central to the Media Industries framework. A media conglomerate typically began as a business in one sector before expanding through purchases and deals. Over time, it acquires interests in television, film studios, music labels, newspapers, digital platforms and merchandise.
