# Widget brief: Trinity as three gods or masks

Cluster id: `trinity-three-persons`  |  serves 7 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

The Trinity is three co-eternal, simultaneously existing persons in one God, not three separate gods or one god wearing different masks in turn.
