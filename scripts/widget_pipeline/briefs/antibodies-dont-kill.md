# Widget brief: Antibodies mark, they don't destroy

Cluster id: `antibodies-dont-kill`  |  serves 4 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Antibodies tag, clump or neutralise pathogens; phagocytes/other cells do the actual destroying.

## The lessons it will sit in

### 1. [science-ocr-b] Immune System, Vaccination and Treating Disease — `/lesson/science-ocr-b/biology-paper-1/5`
Triage: Students think antibodies directly kill pathogens, rather than understanding that antibodies mark, clump, or neutralise—leaving phagocytes or other cells to do the actual destruction.

Understanding how this system works explains both why vaccines are so effective and why antibiotic resistance is so dangerous.
