# Widget brief: Greenhouse gases absorb and re-emit infrared radiation in all directions, not trap it like a blanket

Cluster id: `greenhouse-effect-reemission-not-blanket`  |  serves 12 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Greenhouse gas molecules absorb infrared radiation and re-emit it randomly in all directions, with some returning to Earth's surface, rather than heat being physically trapped or blocked.
