> Power and Authority: Key Concepts In sociology, power and authority are closely related but distinct concepts. Power refers to the capacity to get others to do what you want, regardless of whether they consent &mdash; this can include coercion, force or threat. Authority refers to power that is considered legitimate: people comply because they believe the person or institution exercising power has the right to do so. A prison guard who uses physical restraint exercises power; a judge who imposes a sentence exercises authority because the sentence is backed by a legal framework that most people accept as legitimate. Max Weber (1947) distinguished three distinct types of authority based on the different grounds on which people accept another&rsquo;s right to exercise power over them.
