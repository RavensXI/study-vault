# Widget brief: Graph plateau means a factor is limiting, not exhausted

Cluster id: `photosynthesis-limiting-factor-plateau`  |  serves 5 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

When a photosynthesis graph plateaus, one factor has become limiting while others are still present in excess — nothing has 'run out' or stopped working

> What is Photosynthesis? Photosynthesis is the process by which green plants and algae produce food. They are the main producers of biomass for life on Earth — almost all food chains begin with a photosynthetic organism. Photosynthesis takes place in the chloroplasts , which contain the green pigment chlorophyll that absorbs light energy. Photosynthesis is an endothermic reaction — it takes in energy from the surroundings in the form of light. This is the opposite of respiration, which is exothermic. > Producers and the Flow of Energy All food chains begin with a producer . Green plants and algae are producers because they capture light energy and use it to manufacture glucose &mdash; a process called photosynthesis . Without producers, no energy would enter the food web and life as we know it could not exist. This makes understanding photosynthesis central to understanding ecosystems. Photosynthesis is an endothermic reaction: it takes in energy from the surroundings rather than releasing it. The light energy absorbed by the green pigment chlorophyll is converted into chemical energy stored in glucose molecules. This stored energy can then be released by respiration &mdash; in the plant itself and in every consumer that feeds on the plant.
