# Widget brief: Synaptic transmission converts electrical to chemical and back

Cluster id: `synapse-electrical-to-chemical`  |  serves 9 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

At a synapse the electrical impulse triggers neurotransmitter release across the gap, which then generates a new electrical impulse in the next neuron, it does not simply jump across like a spark.
