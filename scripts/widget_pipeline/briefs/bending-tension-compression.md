# Widget brief: Bending combines simultaneous tension and compression

Cluster id: `bending-tension-compression`  |  serves 3 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Bending a material creates simultaneous tension on one face and compression on the opposite face, not a single bending force.
