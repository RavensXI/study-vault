# Widget brief: ADSR parameters act together, not in sequence

Cluster id: `adsr-simultaneous-shaping`  |  serves 1 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Attack, decay, sustain and release interact simultaneously to shape a sound's evolving envelope, rather than being four separate consecutive events.

## The lessons it will sit in

### 1. [music-technology] Methods of Sound Creation — `/lesson/music-technology/sound-creation/2`
Triage: Students think ADSR is a sequential list of four separate events, rather than understanding that all four parameters interact simultaneously to shape how a sound evolves from the moment a key is pressed to long after it is released.

> Five Methods of Sound Creation Once you know what types of sound are needed for a production, you need to understand how those sounds are made. There are five main methods of sound creation, ranging from entirely physical techniques to fully digital processes. A professional sound designer will use a combination of all five to build a complete soundtrack. Method 1: Physical Props Physical props are real objects used to create sounds. This method is at the heart of Foley work. The process involves two stages: creation of props (finding or building suitable objects) and capture and editing of audio (recording the sounds and processing them to fit the production). For example, to create the sound of walking on snow, a Foley artist might squeeze a bag of cornflour. To simulate breaking bones, they might snap celery sticks.
