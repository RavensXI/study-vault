# Widget brief: Natural selection is not a deliberate/needs-based process

Cluster id: `natural-selection-not-directed`  |  serves 5 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Variation already exists before selection; organisms don't consciously try to adapt or gain traits because they are needed, and 'fittest' means best suited to environment, not strongest.

> Variation Individuals within a species are not identical — they show variation . Some variation is genetic (caused by different alleles, e.g. blood type), some is environmental (caused by living conditions, e.g. a scar), and much is a combination of both (e.g. height is influenced by genes but also by diet). Genetic variation arises through mutations (random changes in DNA), meiosis (shuffling of chromosomes during gamete formation) and sexual reproduction (combining alleles from two parents) (B5.2a). Variation within a population is essential for natural selection because it provides a range of characteristics on which selection can act. Without variation, all individuals would be equally suited (or unsuited) to their environment. Key Fact Variation can be genetic, environmental or both.
