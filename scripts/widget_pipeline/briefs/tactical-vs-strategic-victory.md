# Widget brief: Winning battles is not winning the war

Cluster id: `tactical-vs-strategic-victory`  |  serves 2 lessons

_Audit cluster vietnam-tactical-vs-political-victory, generalised so one mechanism serves both wars._

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Battlefield success and strategic victory are different scoreboards. Each side has its own victory condition and often a clock: Germany’s 1918 Spring Offensive gained more ground than any attack since 1914 yet failed strategically because it did not achieve a decisive result before American strength arrived — and the gains left long, indefensible lines. US search-and-destroy in Vietnam won engagements and body counts, yet the NLF’s condition was to NOT LOSE until American political will broke. The widget must make the two scoreboards concrete: the tactical metric can climb while the strategic position worsens. Build once — the two wars are scenario data for the same mechanism.

American commanders measured the war in firepower, miles of road controlled and enemy bodies counted. The Vietcong measured it in patience, terrain and political legitimacy.
