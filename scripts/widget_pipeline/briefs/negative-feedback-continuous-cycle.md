# Widget brief: Negative feedback as a continuous oscillating cycle

Cluster id: `negative-feedback-continuous-cycle`  |  serves 4 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Negative feedback loops continuously correct deviations around a setpoint and switch off once corrected, repeating as needed, rather than being a single one-off correction.

> What is Homeostasis? Homeostasis is how your body keeps its internal conditions within narrow, safe limits. Enzymes and metabolic reactions function best at specific temperatures, pH levels and ion concentrations &mdash; drift too far from these ideal conditions and cells stop working efficiently, or stop working altogether. Every homeostatic system has three components working together: receptors detect a change; a coordination centre (such as the brain or pancreas) processes the information; effectors (muscles or glands) produce the corrective response. All these systems use the same underlying control mechanism: negative feedback . When a condition rises above its set point, the body acts to bring it down.
