# Widget brief: Respiration, not uneaten material, is the main energy loss between trophic levels

Cluster id: `biomass-transfer-respiration`  |  serves 5 lessons

## The one thing the widget must make concrete

Most biomass/energy loss between trophic levels is due to respiration (heat loss from movement/life processes), not simply parts of organisms being uneaten.

Triage: Students think energy simply moves along a food chain like a baton pass, rather than understanding that most energy is lost (as heat, movement, inedible parts) at each transfer, making higher trophic levels progressively smaller in total biomass.

> Trophic Levels and Food Chains In any ecosystem, energy flows from the Sun through living organisms. The position an organism occupies in a food chain is called its trophic level . Every food chain begins with a producer — an organism (usually a green plant or alga) that converts light energy into chemical energy through photosynthesis . Organisms that eat producers are called primary consumers (trophic level 2). Those that eat primary consumers are secondary consumers (trophic level 3). Tertiary consumers eat secondary consumers and sit at trophic level 4 or above. Key Fact Trophic levels: Level 1 = Producers (plants) → Level 2 = Primary consumers (herbivores) → Level 3 = Secondary consumers (carnivores) → Level 4 = Tertiary consumers (top predators).
