Recall appeals

When a student types an answer to a flashcard, the judge marks it right, nearly or not quite. A student who presses I think I was right? on a not-quite card lands here, with what they typed. Cards with the most appeals first. Nothing here says who appealed. If the typed answers look right, the judge's rule for that card kind needs loosening; if they look wrong, the card is doing its job.
