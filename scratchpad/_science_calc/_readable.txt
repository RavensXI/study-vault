  HINT: Moles of NaOH, halve for the 2:1 ratio, then divide by the acid volume.
   MIS mole_ratio expect=0.25 | The ratio is 1:2 (H₂SO₄ : NaOH). Moles NaOH = 0.2 × 0.025 = 0.005. Moles H₂SO₄ = 0.005 ÷ 2 = 0.0025. Conc = 0.0025 ÷ 0.02 = 0.125 mol/dm³.
   MIS forgot_ratio expect=0.5 | One mole of H₂SO₄ reacts with two moles of NaOH, so you need HALF as many moles of acid: divide by 2, do not multiply. Doubling gives 0.5 mol/dm³, which is wrong.
