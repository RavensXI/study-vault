EMDASH pd.method_card.steps[0] :: Read the question — is the temperature changing or is the state changing (or both)?
EMDASH pd.method_card.content :: <p>This lesson covers <strong>three equations</strong>. Density is a <strong>recall equation</strong> — you must memorise it. SHC and latent heat are <strong>given on the equation sheet</strong>.</p><p><strong>Key strategy — is the temperature changing or is the state changing?</strong></p><ul><li>If the temperature is going up or down → use specific heat capacity</li><li>If ice is melting, water is boiling, or any state change is happening → use latent heat</li><li>If a substance heats up AND changes state, you need <em>two separate calculations added together</em></li></ul><p><strong>Common pitfalls:</strong></p><ul><li>Always convert <strong>grams to kilograms</strong> before substituting (÷ 1000)</li><li>For density, watch the volume units — cm³ and m³ give different density units (g/cm³ vs kg/m³)</li><li>Temperature <em>change</em> means final minus initial — not just the final temperature</li><li>During a state change, temperature stays constant — don't add a temperature change for that part</li><li>Latent heat of fusion (melting) and vaporisation (boiling) are very different values — read the question carefully</li></ul>

EMDASH pd.problem_bank.gold[0].misconceptions[0].message :: Two steps needed. Step 1 — melt the ice: E₁ = mL = 0.3 × 334,000 = 100,200 J. Step 2 — heat the water: E₂ = mcΔθ = 0.3 × 4200 × 40 = 50,400 J. Total = 100,200 + 50,400 = 150,600 J.
EMDASH pd.problem_bank.gold[0].misconceptions[1].message :: Melting uses latent heat (E = mL), heating uses SHC (ΔE = mcΔθ). You need both — add them together.
EMDASH pd.problem_bank.gold[1].misconceptions[0].message :: Step 1 — heat to 100°C: E₁ = mcΔθ = 0.5 × 4200 × 80 = 168,000 J. Step 2 — boil: E₂ = mL = 0.5 × 2,260,000 = 1,130,000 J. Total = 168,000 + 1,130,000 = 1,298,000 J.
EMDASH pd.problem_bank.bronze[4].misconceptions[0].message :: E = m × L = 0.5 × 334,000 = 167,000 J. Don't divide — multiply mass by the specific latent heat.
EMDASH pd.problem_bank.bronze[5].misconceptions[0].message :: This is a state change (boiling), so use E = mL. E = 0.1 × 2,260,000 = 226,000 J. Don't use SHC — the temperature isn't changing during boiling.
