
=== bronze: Bronze: one formula, one step
  STEP: <strong>Pick the formula</strong> that links what you know to what you want: moles = concentration × volume, concentration = moles ÷ volume, or moles = mass ÷ Mr.
  STEP: <strong>Convert the volume to dm³ first</strong> by dividing cm³ by 1000. This is where most marks are lost.
  STEP: Substitute your numbers, work it out, and state the unit: mol, mol/dm³, or dm³.
  EX Q: Calculate the moles in 40 cm³ of a 0.2 mol/dm³ solution.
    - Convert the volume: <p>40 ÷ 1000 = 0.04 dm³</p> [ans=None]
    - Substitute: <p>moles = 0.2 × 0.04 = 0.008 mol</p> [ans=None]
    - Check: <p>0.008 ÷ 0.04 = 0.2 mol/dm³, the concentration we started with</p> [ans=None]
    - Answer: <p><strong>0.008 mol</strong></p> [ans=True]

=== silver: Silver: one titration, one ratio
  STEP: <strong>Find the moles of the solution you fully know</strong> (concentration × volume in dm³).
  STEP: <strong>Use the balanced equation's ratio</strong> to turn those moles into moles of the other substance. The ratio is not always 1:1.
  STEP: Divide by the other solution's volume in dm³ to get its concentration.
  EX Q: 25.0 cm³ of 0.1 mol/dm³ HCl neutralises 20.0 cm³ of NaOH (1:1). Find the NaOH concentration.
    - Moles of HCl: <p>0.1 × 0.025 = 0.0025 mol</p> [ans=None]
    - Ratio: <p>1 : 1, so moles NaOH = 0.0025 mol</p> [ans=None]
    - Concentration: <p>0.0025 ÷ 0.02 = 0.125 mol/dm³</p> [ans=None]
    - Check: <p>0.125 × 0.02 = 0.0025 mol NaOH, matches</p> [ans=None]
    - Answer: <p><strong>0.125 mol/dm³</strong></p> [ans=True]

=== gold: Gold: chain the steps
  STEP: <strong>Work in stages.</strong> If you are given a mass, turn it into moles with moles = mass ÷ Mr first.
  STEP: A sample is often taken from a larger flask. The concentration is the same throughout, so <strong>scale the moles up</strong> to the whole flask at the end.
  STEP: Chain the steps (moles, ratio, concentration or mass) and track the unit on every line.
  EX Q: 2.0 g of NaOH (Mr = 40) is made up to 200 cm³. Find the concentration in the flask.
    - Moles: <p>2.0 ÷ 40 = 0.05 mol</p> [ans=None]
    - Convert the volume: <p>200 ÷ 1000 = 0.2 dm³</p> [ans=None]
    - Concentration: <p>0.05 ÷ 0.2 = 0.25 mol/dm³</p> [ans=None]
    - Check: <p>0.25 × 0.2 = 0.05 mol, matches</p> [ans=None]
    - Answer: <p><strong>0.25 mol/dm³</strong></p> [ans=True]
