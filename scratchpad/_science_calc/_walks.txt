========= OPENER =========
display: Picture a stack of identical books on a table. Each book weighs 15 N.
### opener
  [0] SAY say='The table holds the whole pile, but each book only feels the weight of the ones stacked above it.' done=None
  [1] BOX pre='Put 3 books in the pile. The bottom book has all three pressing down: 3 × 15 = ' post='N' answer=45 phase=None hint='Three books, 15 N each.' done=None say=None
  [2] BOX pre='Now make it 6 books. The bottom feels 6 × 15 = ' post='N' answer=90 phase=None hint='Six books this time.' done=None say=None
  [3] SAY say="Deeper in the pile means more weight pressing down. Water is the same: the deeper you go, the more fluid is stacked above, so the pressure climbs. That is exactly \\(p = h\\rho g\\): pressure grows with depth h, with the fluid's density ρ, and with g." done=None
