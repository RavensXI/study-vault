method_card content pre:
<p><strong>Bar charts:</strong> Height = frequency. <strong>Pie charts:</strong> Angle = (frequency/total) × 360°. <strong>Scatter graphs:</strong> Show correlation (positive, negative, none). <strong>Line of best fit:</strong> Goes through the middle of the data.</p>

method_card content live:
<p><strong>Pie charts:</strong> a sector's angle over 360 is its fraction of the total. Frequency = (angle ÷ 360) × total; angle = (frequency ÷ total) × 360. All sectors add to 360°.</p><p><strong>Scatter graphs:</strong> positive correlation means both rise together; negative means one rises as the other falls. Read the line of best fit to estimate, but only trust estimates inside the data range.</p>
