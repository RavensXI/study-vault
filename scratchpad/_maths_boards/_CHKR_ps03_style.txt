
=== tier_guide gold: title='Gold: composite charts, density and lines of best fit'
  steps(3, wordcount=59): <strong>Composite (stacked) bars:</strong> read each part, add them for the total, then one part as a percentage: part ÷ total × 100. || <strong>Frequency density:</strong> for a histogram bar, frequency density = frequency ÷ class width. || <strong>Line of best fit:</strong> read straight up from the x-value to estimate y; inside the data range (interpolation) is reliable, outside (extrapolation) is not.
  example.q: A stacked bar shows A = 30, B = 20 in one month. What percentage is A?
    - Total: 30 + 20 = 50 [isAnswer=None/None]
    - Percentage: (30 ÷ 50) × 100 = 60% [isAnswer=None/None]
    - Check: 60% of 50 = 30 [isAnswer=None/None]
    - Answer: 60% [isAnswer=True/True]

=== tier_guide bronze: title='Bronze: reading charts and simple pie sectors'
  steps(3, wordcount=69): <strong>Bar charts:</strong> follow the top of a bar across to the number axis. Each gridline is worth the scale shown, so a bar 4 gridlines up on a scale of 5 means 20. || <strong>Pie sectors:</strong> a sector's share is its frequency over the total. Turn a sector into people with (angle ÷ 360) × total. || For 'how many more', read both values, then subtract the smaller from the larger.
  example.q: A bar chart shows Green = 15, Blue = 25. How many more chose blue?
    - Read: Green = 15, Blue = 25 [isAnswer=None/None]
    - Subtract: 25 − 15 = 10 [isAnswer=None/None]
    - Check: 15 + 10 = 25 [isAnswer=None/None]
    - Answer: 10 more chose blue [isAnswer=True/True]

=== tier_guide silver: title='Silver: comparing data, pie angles and two-way tables'
  steps(4, wordcount=60): <strong>Angle to frequency:</strong> divide the angle by 360 to get the fraction, then multiply by the total. || <strong>Frequency to angle:</strong> divide the frequency by the total, then multiply by 360. || <strong>Missing angle:</strong> all sectors add to 360°, so subtract the known angles from 360. || <strong>Two-way tables:</strong> each row and column adds to its total, so fill a gap by subtracting.
  example.q: A pie chart shows 90 people. Sector B is 40°. How many people is that?
    - Fraction: 40 ÷ 360 = 1/9 [isAnswer=None/None]
    - Multiply: 1/9 × 90 = 10 [isAnswer=None/None]
    - Check: (10 ÷ 90) × 360 = 40° [isAnswer=None/None]
    - Answer: 10 people [isAnswer=True/True]
