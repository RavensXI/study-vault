--- bronze[0] input_type=single_value calculator=False chart=False

SOLUTIONS: [2]

  MISC[0] expect=0.5 pattern=rise_run_inverted msg=That is run ÷ rise. Gradient is rise ÷ run: (11 − 3) ÷ (5 − 1) = 8 ÷ 4 = 2.

===== SILVER (7 problems) desc: Iterate with fraction or surd formulas and round to given decimal places, keeping full accuracy between steps.

  MISC[0] expect=4.667 pattern=single_denominator msg=The denominator is 2x, not x: 14 ÷ (2 × 3) = 14 ÷ 6 = 2.333, not 14 ÷ 3.
