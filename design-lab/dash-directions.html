Five directions for the student home

Same jobs in every one — start today’s plan, pick a subject, lean back — same type, same palette, same art language. What changes is the organising idea. Click any card to open it full-size.

Direction oneThe Almanac

Today’s front page. A dated masthead, a serif lead, the week’s plate as a captioned figure, and The Index · Pictures · The Wireless across the foot.

 Direction twoThe Landscape

No page at all — the panorama is the dashboard. The plan is a sheet pinned in the sky, everything else is written on the sky, and the ground belongs to the artist.

 Direction threeThe Notebook

An open journal: the plan on a ruled left page, the week’s art tipped in with tape, shorts as polaroids — and the nine subjects as cloth tabs on the fore-edge.

 Direction fourThe Poster

Type is the design. Tonight’s ask set enormous — Twenty-five minutes. — with the programme as a fine table of contents and the week reduced to a postage stamp.

 Direction fiveThe Desk

Sam’s desk as the interface: the plan on a jotter, a tear-off exam calendar, a drawn wireless set for Revision Radio, polaroid shorts, and subjects as book spines on the shelf.

 ReferenceLast night’s baseline

The sentence layout from before bed, kept for comparison — plan card with full-ink plate, clause bands below.
