A symphony that starts in the wrong key

The movement begins with a slow Adagio molto introduction . The very first chord is a dominant seventh — but it belongs to F major, not the home key of C. Beethoven delays confirming the tonic, so that when C major finally arrives at the Allegro con brio , it lands with far more force.

Sonata form across the Allegro

The exposition presents two contrasting ideas: the driving first subject built from short repeated motifs and sforzando accents, then a second subject in the related key , where the wind instruments carry their own melodic material. Classical convention then repeats the whole exposition .

The development pushes fragments of those themes through new keys — the least stable music in the movement — before the recapitulation returns everything home to C major. A coda rounds the movement off with emphatic cadences.

Orchestration: Beethoven and the wind

Pairs of flutes, oboes, clarinets and bassoons, horns, trumpets, timpani and strings — but listen to how the wind section gets its own material rather than doubling the strings, especially at the second subject .
