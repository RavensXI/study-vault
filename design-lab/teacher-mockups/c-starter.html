1 of 3 Conflict and Tension 1918–39 · lesson 3 11 of the 19 who met it got this wrong

Which clause of the Treaty of Versailles did Germans call the “War Guilt Clause”?

Ask them first. Then show what the class actually said.

Eleven of you said
Clause 232 — the reparations clause
Close, and a real clause — but 232 is the bill, not the blame.
The answer
Article 231
It made Germany accept responsibility for the war. 231 comes first because the guilt is what 232 then charges for.
