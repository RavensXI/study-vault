Line & wash — Flash v1 / Flash v2 / Pro

Same source hero, same run. Flash v1 = original prompt. Flash v2 = much stronger "hand-drawn ink, no photo texture, no text" prompt. Pro = Nano Banana Pro. The question was whether a stronger prompt makes Flash match Pro.

Verdict: it doesn't. The stronger prompt left the hard cases failing (micrograph stays photographic, treaty keeps its text) and destabilised the good ones — the Ford line lost its watercolour and went monochrome ink, Macbeth went more photographic. The Flash↔Pro gap is a model limit, not a prompt limit. (The single-person portrait was refused by both models — a sourcing rule.)
