# StudyVault Design Constitution
*One page. Every screen gets judged against this, so taste decisions become
checklist decisions. Extracted from what Tom has already approved — nothing
here is new, it's just finally written down. (5 Jul 2026)*

## The thesis
**The world is painted; the reading is print.** Two committed modes, never a
half-blend: the desk is a place (diegetic, illustrated, playful); classic and
all reading surfaces are a beautifully-made system (crisp, warm, instant).

## The laws
1. **One screen.** A home surface fits the viewport. No scrolling to know
   where you stand; certainly no long lists. Depth is *on demand* (expand,
   modal, navigate) — never by default.
2. **Dual-code everything.** Every number gets a shape (bar, arc, box-fill,
   calendar mark). Every subject speaks in its cloth colour before its name.
   If information appears only as words, ask why.
3. **Few elements, earning rent.** Each element must change a decision or a
   feeling. (Radio/Next Mock failed this test and died. Apply the same test
   forever.) When in doubt, remove one thing — then look again.
4. **One verb grammar.** Lift = pick-uppable/openable. Underline = go
   somewhere. Ink ring = THE one recommended action (max one per screen).
   Nothing else may borrow these signals.
5. **Still at rest, juicy on change.** Animate deltas caused by the student
   (bar fills +2%, card graduates); never blanket-reveal a page. Sub-200ms
   transitions; entrance theatre only where it tells a story (desk curtain).
6. **The accent is a thread, not a paint job.** Constant warm chrome;
   subject colour appears in small, meaningful places (tab, hairline, scrim,
   progress). Print stays print-coloured.
7. **Type: two voices + one hand.** Display (Schibsted) for structure, serif
   (Literata) for reading, Caveat ONLY as annotation — a human margin note,
   max ~one per zone. Never UI copy in the hand; never handwriting fonts
   plural.
8. **Buttons look like buttons** where the mode is system; in the painted
   world, affordances are objects. No half-measures in either direction.
9. **Ends exist.** Sessions are bounded; done-states are designed ("done for
   today"). We tell students when to stop. Empty states teach how to begin.
10. **Honest surfaces.** Labels match the data (lessons are "lessons"); zeros
    show as zeros with a path forward; nothing fakes activity.

## Why this is the moat
Bitesize and Seneca ship functional-but-generic. A product teens are *proud
to be seen using* — coherent, crafted, calm — is a defensible advantage that
content parity can't erase. Getting it right = applying this page, not
reinventing taste per screen.

## Process (how we work, so nobody drowns)
- Claude generates against the constitution and brings ONE screen at a time,
  rendered, not five directions in the abstract.
- Tom is the taste gate: yes / no / adjust. Reactions to concrete screens
  only — never asked to imagine from prose.
- Violations of a law need a written excuse in the commit message or they're
  bugs.
