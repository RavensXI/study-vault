## 5. Food Preparation and Nutrition (AQA 8585) -- 10 lessons, 1 unit

### Spec mapping

| Spec section | Required content | Lesson(s) | Covered? |

| 3.2.2 Micronutrients | 3.2.2.1 Vitamins (A, D, E, K, B group, C; antioxidants; cooking effects), 3.2.2.2 Minerals (calcium, iron, sodium, fluoride, iodine, phosphorus), 3.2.2.3 Water | L3 Micronutrients: Vitamins, L4 Micronutrients: Minerals and Water | YES |
| 3.2.3 Nutritional needs & health | Eatwell Guide, balanced diets for life stages, dietary groups (vegetarian, vegan, coeliac, lactose intolerant), BMI, energy balance | L5 The Eatwell Guide and Balanced Diets, L6 Energy Needs and Nutritional Analysis | YES |

| 3.5 Food choice | 3.5.1 Factors affecting food choice (income, lifestyle, religion, culture, medical, ethical), food labelling, marketing | L10 Food Choice and Food Labelling | YES |

### Content spot-check

**L1 (Macronutrients: Protein):** Covers amino acids (20 total, 8 essential), HBV and LBV proteins with correct definitions, protein complementation, protein alternatives (TVP, soya, mycoprotein/Quorn, tofu). Functions (growth, repair, maintenance, secondary energy, enzymes/hormones/antibodies). Sources properly categorised. Deficiency and excess effects covered. Dietary reference values mentioned. **Accurate and thorough. Matches spec 3.2.1.1.**

**L8 (Food Spoilage, Contamination and Poisoning):** Covers bacteria, moulds, yeasts as microorganisms. Signs of spoilage (colour, texture, smell, taste, visible mould). Conditions for bacterial growth (warmth 5-63C danger zone, moisture, food, time, pH, oxygen). High-risk vs low-risk foods. Food contamination types (physical, chemical, biological). Named bacteria (Salmonella, E. coli, Campylobacter, Staphylococcus aureus, Listeria). Food poisoning symptoms and prevention. **Accurate and matches spec 3.4.1.**

### Issues found

1. **COVERAGE GAP -- Food Science (spec section 3.3) is not explicitly covered as a standalone lesson.** The spec requires detailed knowledge of: why food is cooked and how heat is transferred (conduction, convection, radiation), functional/chemical properties of proteins (denaturation, coagulation, gluten formation, foam formation), carbohydrates (dextrinisation, caramelisation, gelatinisation), fats (shortening, aeration, plasticity, emulsification), and raising agents (biological, chemical, steam). Some of these concepts may be touched on within existing lessons (e.g. protein denaturation in L1, gelatinisation in L2), but there is no dedicated food science lesson covering heat transfer methods, the Maillard reaction, or the detailed functional properties systematically.

2. **COVERAGE GAP -- Food Provenance (spec section 3.6) has no dedicated lesson.** The spec requires knowledge of: food sources (agriculture, farming methods, organic vs conventional, GM foods, sustainability), food and the environment (food miles, carbon footprint, seasonal eating, sustainability, Fairtrade), and food processing/production (primary and secondary processing, fortification, modified starches). None of the 10 lesson titles suggest this is covered.

3. **MINOR -- Food Choice (L10) may be thin for spec 3.5.** The spec section covers not only food choice factors and labelling but also British and international cuisines (food traditions from different cultures, how food choices have changed over time, fusion foods). This is a substantial topic area that may be compressed into a single lesson alongside labelling.

### Verdict: PARTIAL PASS

The 10 lessons cover sections 3.2 (nutrition) and 3.4 (food safety) well, plus food choice (3.5) in L10. However, **food science (3.3) and food provenance (3.6) are absent as dedicated topics**. These are two of the five core content sections in the AQA 8585 spec and are examined in the written paper. This is the most significant coverage gap found across all five subjects.

**Recommendation:** Add 2-4 lessons to fill the gaps:
- L11: Food Science -- Heat Transfer & Cooking Methods
- L12: Food Science -- Functional Properties of Ingredients
- L13: Food Provenance -- Food Sources & the Environment
- L14: Food Provenance -- Food Processing & Production
