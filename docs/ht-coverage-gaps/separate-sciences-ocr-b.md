#### Key Decisions and Rationale

1. **Conservative wrapping:** Only whole `<p data-narration-id>` elements and collapsible/key-fact block elements were wrapped. No prose was modified. Where HT content was embedded mid-paragraph alongside Foundation content, it was flagged as a gap rather than split.

2. **Biological methods (C3.2 #6):** The spec verb is "evaluate" (HT). The lesson presents both phytomining and bioleaching with advantages and disadvantages. The entire section was wrapped, not just the evaluative sentences, because the descriptive content only appears in this HT context — Foundation students get the concept of reduction/smelting, not the biological alternatives.
