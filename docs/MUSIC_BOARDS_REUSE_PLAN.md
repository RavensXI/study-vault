# Music: the other boards — reuse plan off the AQA build

**Written 16 Aug 2026, from the current specs in `specs/` (currency checked:
Eduqas's post-2022 extract change is reflected — Badinerie + Africa are the
live prepared extracts; Edexcel Issue 4, Dec 2021; no reform pending for
any board's 2027 series).** Companion to the PIPELINE.md boundary note: the
expensive layer of Music AQA was instrument-building, and instruments
transfer.

## What transfers to EVERY board at zero cost

The platform layer, 100% reuse, no work beyond wiring:

- the score player (dynamics/articulation performed), verovio engraving,
  waveform players, annotated study-piece player
- the tier system with two-attempt cap + worked-example interstitials
- misconception machinery (per-distractor diagnoses pipeline + validators)
- the guards built this week: ear-vs-fact validator, passage-id leak check,
  practice QA gates, YouTube link audit
- the licence-clean recording library: Beethoven Sym 1, Mozart 40 + K.622,
  Haydn 94, Handel Zadok, Chopin Op.9/2, Schumann Träumerei, Verdi Dies
  irae, plus the whole synthesised drill bank (metre, tonality, cadence,
  texture, device, pop)
- narration, podcast, related-media and hero pipelines

**The skills units copy nearly verbatim** (free-tier → free-tier, so no
Unity-porting issue): `listening-skills` (3 lessons) and `score-reading`
(4 lessons) are board-agnostic musicianship every board's appraising paper
examines. Per-board cost: relabelling + a terminology pass (and Eduqas's
neutral-phrasing rule).

## Build order and effort

**Eduqas → OCR → Edexcel.** Rationale: descending reuse, ascending
licensing burden, and Eduqas earns the Wales bridge. With the AQA formats
and validators in place, each style-based board is **days not weeks**, with
Tom's involvement limited to: recording choices (ear + licence), the
standard review pass, and podcast/NLM batches.

What can be built WITHOUT Tom in an overnight/day session per board:
unit plans, all article prose (fact-check gated), drills reusing EXISTING
verified recordings and synthesis, worked examples, misconception drafts,
question banks through the QA gates. What waits for him: anything with a
NEW recording, and the review flip.
