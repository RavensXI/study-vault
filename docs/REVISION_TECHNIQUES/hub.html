Foundation Techniques

Start early — use these from day one
Active recall

Retrieval Practice

Test yourself, don’t just re-read. Brain dumps and self-quizzing beat highlighting every time.

 Distributed practice

Spaced Repetition

Revisit the same material at 1, 3, 7, 14, 30-day gaps. Forgets less than cramming. Works better than anything.

 Visual learning

Dual Coding

Combine words and visuals. Timelines, flowcharts and comparison tables stick better than text alone.

 Deep thinking

Elaborative Interrogation

Ask “why?” and “how?” to deepen understanding. Turn memorised facts into understood explanations.

Consolidation Techniques

Use these once you know the basics
Mixed practice

Interleaving

Mix related topics in one session. Feels harder. Exam-ready faster. Trains you to identify which technique to use.

 Structured notes

Knowledge Organisers

One page per topic. Redraw from memory. The base layer of every good revision routine.

Timed Practice
