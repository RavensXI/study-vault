How to Solve by Factorising

A quadratic equation has the form \(ax^2 + bx + c = 0\). To solve it by factorising, you rewrite the left side as a product of two brackets, then use the zero product property: if \(A \times B = 0\), then either \(A = 0\) or \(B = 0\).

  1. Rearrange the equation so one side equals zero.
  2. Factorise the quadratic into two brackets: \((x + p)(x + q) = 0\).
  3. Set each bracket equal to zero and solve for \(x\).
  4. Write both solutions clearly.
Worked Example
$$x^2 + 5x + 6 = 0$$

Find two numbers that multiply to give \(+6\) and add to give \(+5\). Those numbers are \(+2\) and \(+3\).

$$(x + 2)(x + 3) = 0$$

Set each bracket to zero:

$$x + 2 = 0 \implies x = -2 \qquad x + 3 = 0 \implies x = -3$$

Solutions: \(x = -2\) or \(x = -3\)

Worked Examples

Try each step yourself on paper first, then reveal to check your working.

1 Solve \(x^2 + 7x + 12 = 0\) Bronze
Step 1 — Identify

We need two numbers that multiply to give \(+12\) and add to give \(+7\).

Step 2 — Find the numbers

Factors of 12: \(1 \times 12\), \(2 \times 6\), \(3 \times 4\). Which pair adds to 7? \(3 + 4 = 7\). The numbers are \(+3\) and \(+4\).

Step 3 — Factorise

$$x^2 + 7x + 12 = (x + 3)(x + 4) = 0$$

Step 4 — Solve
Answer

$$x + 3 = 0 \implies x = -3 \qquad x + 4 = 0 \implies x = -4$$

Solutions: \(x = -3\) or \(x = -4\)

2 Solve \(x^2 - x - 12 = 0\) Silver
Step 1 — Identify

Two numbers that multiply to give \(-12\) and add to give \(-1\). Since the product is negative, one number is positive and one is negative.

Step 2 — Find the numbers

Factor pairs of 12: \(1 \times 12\), \(2 \times 6\), \(3 \times 4\). We need them to add to \(-1\), so try \(+3\) and \(-4\): \(3 + (-4) = -1\) and \(3 \times (-4) = -12\).

Step 3 — Factorise

$$x^2 - x - 12 = (x + 3)(x - 4) = 0$$

Step 4 — Solve
Answer

$$x + 3 = 0 \implies x = -3 \qquad x - 4 = 0 \implies x = 4$$

Solutions: \(x = 4\) or \(x = -3\)

3 Solve \(x^2 - 9 = 0\) Silver
Step 1 — Recognise the pattern

This is a difference of two squares: \(x^2 - 9 = x^2 - 3^2\). There is no \(x\) term (i.e. \(b = 0\)).

Step 2 — Factorise

$$x^2 - 9 = (x + 3)(x - 3) = 0$$

Step 3 — Solve
Answer

$$x + 3 = 0 \implies x = -3 \qquad x - 3 = 0 \implies x = 3$$

Solutions: \(x = 3\) or \(x = -3\)

4 Solve \(x^2 = 5x - 6\) Gold
Step 1 — Rearrange

Move everything to one side so it equals zero. Subtract \(5x\) and add \(6\):

$$x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$$

Step 2 — Find the numbers

Two numbers that multiply to give \(+6\) and add to give \(-5\). Both must be negative: \(-2\) and \(-3\).

Step 3 — Factorise

$$x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x - 2)(x - 3) = 0$$

Step 4 — Solve
Answer

$$x - 2 = 0 \implies x = 2 \qquad x - 3 = 0 \implies x = 3$$

Solutions: \(x = 2\) or \(x = 3\)
