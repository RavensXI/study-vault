# Highlight Mode — 15-second demo script

Recommended storyboard for a short social video demonstrating the feature.

## Format

* **Duration:** 12–15 seconds total. Aim shorter rather than longer — most social feeds cut off engagement at 10s.
* **Aspect ratio:** 1:1 (square) for LinkedIn / Twitter feed, or 9:16 (vertical) for Instagram Reels / TikTok / YouTube Shorts. Avoid 16:9 unless it's specifically for YouTube or a website embed — feeds crop it badly.
* **Sound:** Mute by default. Captions must carry the message. Optionally an unobtrusive lo-fi loop under it; no voice-over needed.

## Shot list

### Shot 1 — Title card (1.5s)

* Plain `#faf8f5` background
* Centered: serif headline **"Highlights that actually stick."**
* Below in smaller Inter: *"Notes you save, ready when you revise."*
* Subtle fade-in, hold

### Shot 2 — The setup (1.5s)

* Cut to the sample-lesson page. Calm, slightly zoomed-in on the article body. Cursor visible.
* Caption: **"Click Highlight Mode"** with an arrow pointing to the bottom-left FAB.
* Cursor moves to the FAB, clicks.

### Shot 3 — Article lifts (1s)

* The article page "lifts" — gains a soft drop shadow, slight scale-up. The cursor becomes a marker pen.
* No caption — let the visual do the work.

### Shot 4 — The highlight (3s)

* Cursor drags across a sentence in the article: e.g. *"around two and a half million East Germans fled west"*.
* As the cursor releases, the highlight sweeps in left-to-right in yellow.
* Caption: **"Drag to highlight"** (fades in, fades out).

### Shot 5 — The popover (2.5s)

* Popover appears above the highlight.
* Quickly: cursor clicks the **green swatch** → the highlight changes colour.
* Cursor clicks into the note field and a typed line appears: *"key statistic — Republikflucht"*.
* Caption: **"Add a colour, add a note"**.

### Shot 6 — Multi-highlight beauty shot (2.5s)

* Quick montage: two more highlights appear elsewhere on the page (a pink one, a blue one), each with its own short note as a faint floating annotation.
* Caption: **"Build your own revision notes as you read"**.

### Shot 7 — The payoff (3s)

* Cursor clicks the bottom-left **Lesson Highlights** button.
* Modal slides up showing the three highlights as a clean list with their notes.
* Cursor hovers over **"Print these notes"** — frame fades.
* Caption: **"Print them. Revise them. Don't lose them."**.

### Shot 8 — End card (1s)

* Plain background, StudyVault wordmark, URL `studyvault.co.uk`.
* Tagline: *"GCSE revision that respects your time."*

## Optional mobile variant (separate 10-second clip)

If you want a dedicated mobile demo:

1. Phone frame, lesson page open. Caption: **"On mobile? Even easier."**
2. Tap a word — the dashed outline appears around it.
3. Caption appears at top of screen: **"Tap the start, tap the end"**.
4. Tap a second word — the highlight sweeps across, popover appears.
5. End card.

This pairs well as a Twitter thread reply to the desktop demo, or as a second LinkedIn carousel slide.
